Hi! I am a PhD student at [Georgia Tech](https://gatech.edu), advised by [Prof. James Hays](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~hays/). My research is in Spatial AI, I train models to generate and reconstruct interactive virtual 3D environments from images. I am currently also a research intern at [Waymo](https://waymo.com/). I enjoy working at the intersection of diffusion models, 3D geometry, and optimization.

I've been fortunate to intern with some outstanding teams at Google DeepMind, Niantic Spatial, and Blue River Technology. Before starting my PhD, I worked on long-range perception at Waymo. During my Master's program, I was introduced to [SfM](https://github.com/borglab/gtsfm) (and 3D vision) at [Prof. Frank Dellaert](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/~dellaert/)'s group. I was also part of a wonderful team from the [RAIL lab](http://rail.gatech.edu/) that won the [FetchIt! challenge](https://youtu.be/vh34WuWuIac) at ICRA 2019.

I have served as a reviewer for CVPR 2025, ICCV 2025, NeurIPS 2026, BMVC 2026, and RA-L. Please feel free to email me if you're interested in research collaborations!
